The showroom

An outlet you can walk through

Mobilia opened in 2015, out of a simple gap in the market: good furniture in Qatar was either expensive or hard to find. The answer was an outlet where you can walk the floor, sit on the pieces and see a few different living styles side by side — for homes and for commercial spaces alike.

If nothing on the floor is quite right, the same pieces can be made to your measurements and your choice of fabric and wood.
